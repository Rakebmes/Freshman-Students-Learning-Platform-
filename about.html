About Us

Freshman Learning Hub is an educational platform created by third year software engineering students to support first-year university students in their academic journey. The platform brings together course information, study materials, practice quizzes, past examination papers, and learning resources in one convenient location.

Starting university can be challenging, and our goal is to make learning easier by providing organized and accessible resources that help students build strong academic foundations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help freshmen succeed by providing reliable learning resources, encouraging independent study, and supporting academic growth through easy access to educational content.

Who Can Use This Platform?

  • First-year university students
  • Students looking for study materials and revision resources
  • Learners preparing for examinations and quizzes
  • Anyone seeking additional educational support
