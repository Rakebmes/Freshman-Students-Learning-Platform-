Academic Calendar 2026/27

This page lists the important dates of the 2026/27 (2019 E.C) academic year at Addis Ababa University: registration, the start and end of each semester, examination periods, breaks, and other university events. Use it to plan your studies and avoid missing key dates.

Please note: These dates are taken from the Office of the Registrar's 2026/27 Academic Calendar. Dates may change, so always check the official university announcements. Assignment deadlines are not listed here because they are different for each course. Please ask your course instructor for those.

For first-year students: Most activities in the official calendar are for Year II and above. Year I students are placed into their programs in February 2027 (see the Placement Period), and Year I registration is scheduled separately.

First Semester

The first semester is sixteen (16) weeks long for students of all years.

Undergraduate Regular Registration 17 - 18 September 2026 Year II and above undergraduate regular students.

Placement Period for Year I Students (February 2027)

This is the most important period for first-year students. You choose your academic program and the university places you in it.

Placement Steps and Dates

Step Date Details
Program Orientation 1 February 2027 Orientation about the available programs, such as Medicine, Dental Medicine, Pharmacy, other Health Sciences, Pre-engineering, Architecture and Built Environment, Computer Science, Information Science, other Natural Sciences, Law and other Social Sciences.
Choose Your Program Preferences 2 - 4 February 2027 Students fill in their program preferences on their portal.

Breaks and University Events

Public Holidays

The official university calendar does not list national or religious public holidays. Public holiday dates are announced separately, so check the official university notices. Dates for religious holidays change from year to year.

Planning Tips for Freshmen

  1. Write the important dates in your own planner or phone calendar.
  2. Register on time so that you do not lose your place in a course.
  3. Learn about the programs before the February placement period, so you can choose well.
  4. Start preparing for examinations at least two weeks early.
  5. Check the official university notice board regularly for changes.

Ready to prepare? Try the Practice Quizzes, review old exams in Past Papers, or read the Study Materials.
